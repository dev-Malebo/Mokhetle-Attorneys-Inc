About The Firm

A firm built on integrity, experience & results.

We provide a full pyramid of legal advisory services — from courtroom litigation to commercial and property advice.

Mokhetle Attorneys Inc is a well-established Mahikeng law firm with experience across litigation, commercial matters, labour law, personal injury claims, property disputes, and more. We've represented individuals, businesses, and public bodies — including appointment by the North West Gambling Board in a procurement matter.

Our approach is simple: warm, accessible legal service backed by deep expertise. Whether you're facing a dispute, drafting a will, or need commercial advice — we're here to guide you clearly, and represent you firmly.
